## 🕹️ Quick Start Guide
1. Download `hyper-screenshots-1.0.0+26.1.2.jar` and place it in your `.minecraft/mods/` folder.
2. Ensure **Fabric API** is present for your Minecraft version.
3. (Optional) Install **ModMenu** and **YetAnotherConfigLib v3 (YACL)** to access the graphical settings screen.
4. Launch the game and press **`F2`** to capture using your configured preset, or **`Ctrl + F2`** for an instant 16K QUHD masterpiece.
